How Image Studio works

  1. Select images from your device or drag them into the upload area.
  2. Set maximum dimensions, output format, compression quality, crop and rotation.
  3. Process the batch and inspect each preview.
  4. Download an individual image or create one ZIP archive for the full batch.

Image decoding, editing and encoding happen in your browser. JSZip is requested from jsDelivr only when you choose ZIP download. Very large batches can use significant memory; start with fewer files on older phones.

Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded?

No. Image files are processed locally in your browser and are not sent to BuyTools.si.

Which formats can I export?

JPEG, PNG, WebP, or the original format where the browser can preserve it. Browser encoder support may vary.

Does resizing preserve the original quality?

Resizing changes pixel dimensions. JPEG and WebP may use lossy compression; PNG is lossless encoding but cannot restore detail removed by resizing.

How many images can I process?

Up to 50 files, each no larger than 20 MB. Practical limits depend on your device memory.
