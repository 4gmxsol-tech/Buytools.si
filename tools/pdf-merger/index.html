How to use this tool

  1. Select two or more PDF files from your device.
  2. Confirm the files are listed in the order you want them combined; the tool merges them in the selected order.
  3. Choose Merge PDF files and save the resulting document, then open it to verify the pages.

For privacy, do not submit sensitive files or secrets to any online tool unless you have reviewed its processing and privacy details. Some BuyTools.si tools load third-party libraries when needed.

Frequently asked questions

Can I merge several PDFs into one?

Yes. Select at least two PDF files and the tool will copy their pages into one output PDF.

Are the files uploaded?

The merger uses a browser-loaded PDF library and processes the files in your browser. The library itself is loaded from a third-party CDN.

Can I merge very large PDFs?

It depends on your device memory and the size of the documents. Try smaller batches if the browser becomes slow.
