Frequently asked questions

What ratio does WCAG AA require for normal text?

WCAG AA generally requires a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text.

Is passing this check enough for accessibility?

No. Contrast is one part of accessibility. Also check focus states, meaningful labels, keyboard use, and other interface requirements.

Does the checker upload my colors?

The calculation runs in your browser. It does not need an account or server-side color processing.
