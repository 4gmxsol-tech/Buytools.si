Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate X percent of a number?

Convert X percent to a decimal by dividing by 100, then multiply by the number.

How is percentage change calculated?

Percentage change is (new value − original value) divided by the absolute original value, multiplied by 100. A zero original value makes the standard calculation undefined.

Can this replace financial advice?

No. It performs arithmetic only. Financial, tax and business decisions may require additional context and professional review.
