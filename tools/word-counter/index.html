  1. Open the word counter.

For best results, use an up-to-date browser. Always review generated files before sharing or publishing them.

Frequently asked questions

Does it count spaces?

The character total includes spaces and line breaks. A separate word count is calculated from whitespace-separated text.

How is reading time estimated?

The tool estimates reading time at about 200 words per minute; actual speed varies by reader and content.

Is the text saved?

The tool processes text in your browser and does not submit it to a BuyTools.si server.
