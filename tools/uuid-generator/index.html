Frequently asked questions

What is a UUID?

A UUID is a 128-bit identifier commonly represented as hexadecimal groups separated by hyphens.

Are UUIDs guaranteed never to collide?

No random identifier scheme can promise zero collisions, but UUID v4 provides a very large random space for common applications.

Should I use a UUID as a secret token?

Do not assume a general-purpose UUID is suitable for every security-sensitive token. Follow the requirements of your authentication or security design.
