How to remove duplicate lines

  1. Paste your text into the tool. Put each item on its own line.
  2. Choose whether matching should ignore case or surrounding spaces.
  3. Select Remove duplicate lines to keep the first occurrence of each unique line.
  4. Copy the clean text or download it as a TXT file.

The tool runs in your browser. Text is not uploaded by this tool.

Useful for SEO keyword lists and content cleanup

SEO research often produces repeated keywords from exports, brainstorming sessions and keyword clustering. Removing exact duplicate lines helps tidy a list before you group terms by intent. It does not determine whether two different phrases have the same search intent; review close variants separately.

It can also clean product lists, names, tags, log snippets and copied data. Keep a copy of the original text when the order or duplicates may be important.

Frequently asked questions

Does it preserve the original order?

Yes. The first occurrence of each matching line stays in its original position.

Can it ignore uppercase and lowercase differences?

Yes. Enable the ignore-case option to treat “Keyword” and “keyword” as duplicates.

Does it upload my text?

No. The tool processes the pasted text locally in your browser.

Will it merge similar keywords?

No. It removes matching duplicate lines only. Similar but different keyword phrases remain separate.
