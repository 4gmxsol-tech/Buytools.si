Convert JPG, PNG and WebP images

Different image formats suit different jobs. JPEG is commonly used for photographs, PNG is useful when transparency or crisp interface graphics matter, and WebP can provide compact images for modern websites. The best choice depends on the content and where you plan to use it.

  1. Choose an image from your device.
  2. Select JPEG, PNG or WebP as the output format.
  3. Convert and inspect the result.
  4. Download the converted image and keep the original until you confirm it looks right.

Which image format should you choose?

  • JPEG: a practical choice for photographs and complex images; it uses lossy compression and does not preserve transparency.
  • PNG: useful for logos, screenshots and graphics that need lossless output or transparency; files can be larger.
  • WebP: a modern web format that supports lossy and lossless compression plus transparency in compatible workflows.

Converting formats does not guarantee a smaller file. Compare output size and visual quality. If you mainly need a smaller photo, try the image compressor.

Local image conversion

The converter uses your browser to decode and re-encode the selected image. Very large images can require substantial memory, especially on phones. Work with a copy of important originals and use smaller files when a device is low on memory.

Frequently asked questions

Can I convert PNG to JPG?

Yes. Choose JPEG as the output format. Transparent areas cannot remain transparent in JPEG and are filled with a solid background.

Can I convert JPG to PNG?

Yes. Choose PNG as the output format. Converting a lossy JPEG to PNG does not restore detail already lost in the original.

Does image conversion always reduce file size?

No. The output size depends on the source format, image content, dimensions and encoder settings.
