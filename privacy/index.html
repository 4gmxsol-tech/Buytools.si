Privacy Policy

Read how BuyTools.si handles browser-based tool inputs, third-party services, advertising and privacy-related requests.

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Overview

BuyTools.si provides browser-based utilities. This policy describes the site's intended data handling. Individual tools can behave differently, so read the notice shown near a tool before using it.

Files and text you enter

Many image and text operations are designed to run locally in your browser and do not require an account. The QR code generator currently sends the entered text or URL to a third-party QR image service to create the image. Some tools may load external libraries or models from third-party hosting. Do not submit confidential documents, passwords, API keys or sensitive personal information to tools unless you have verified the relevant processing behavior.

Hosting and technical data

The site is hosted as a static website. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address, request time, browser details and requested paths for security, delivery and troubleshooting under its own policies.

Advertising and analytics

Advertising and analytics may be introduced as the site develops. If third-party advertising is enabled, providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, limit or measure ads, subject to their policies and applicable law. This page should be updated to name the actual providers and describe any consent controls before those services are enabled. We do not claim that advertising is currently configured just because this policy describes possible future use.

External services

Links, libraries and services provided by third parties are governed by their own privacy terms. BuyTools.si does not control their independent practices.

Your choices and contact

You can avoid entering sensitive information, disable browser storage, and use browser controls to manage site data. For privacy questions, contact the project through the public issue tracker; do not post private personal details there. Applicable privacy rights depend on your location and the services in use.

Policy changes

This policy may change as tools, hosting, analytics or monetization features change. The updated date above should be revised when material changes are made.
