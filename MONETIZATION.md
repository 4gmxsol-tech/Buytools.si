# BuyTools.si monetization policy

## Current position

No advertising network is active on BuyTools.si. The production build does not inject ad scripts or placements. There are no Adsterra units, Smartlinks, Popunders, Social Bars, affiliate redirects or advertising pixels in the site's monetization setup.

Advertising was removed because the delivered creatives and landing pages could include misleading promotions and betting-related material. Protecting visitor trust and the site's reputation takes priority over short-term advertising revenue.

## Future review gate

Do not add an advertising provider or monetization script without a separate explicit review. That review must inspect actual creatives and destination behavior across desktop and mobile, verify provider and publisher-account legitimacy, assess privacy and consent requirements, and confirm that gambling, deceptive claims, forced redirects, downloads and intrusive formats can be blocked reliably.

No advertising revenue, approval status, ad impressions or RPM is claimed.

## Other product considerations

The Background Removal feature loads `@imgly/background-removal@1.5.5`. The upstream project identifies this package as AGPL-3.0. Its licensing and network-use obligations remain a separate product/legal review item; removing advertising does not resolve that question.
